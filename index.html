How to play

Throw

Hold the left mouse button, pull the mouse back to wind up, then flick it forward. The pull sets your power; a crisp, straight flick delivers it. Drifting sideways pulls the throw off line.

Aim

A D turn · W S loft · hold Shift for fine steps. Right-drag also turns and lofts.

Shape

Q E or the mouse wheel (3° a notch, 1° with Shift) tilts the disc: hyzer curves towards the fade side, anhyzer away from it. R F raise or drop the nose.

Bag

1 driver · 2 midrange · 3 putter. T switches backhand, forehand and overhand. G switches full, approach and putt swings.

Views

M overhead map · Tab scorecard · H this help · N music on/off. The dashed line shows a full swing in still air; wind is on you.

Rules

Streets and yards are in play, and so are docks, bridges and decks. Roofs, pools, ponds, lakes and creeks are out of bounds: one penalty throw, then play from where the disc last crossed in. Farthest from the basket throws next.
